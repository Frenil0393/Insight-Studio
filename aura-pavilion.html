01The context

Responding
to the place.

A large-scale public art venue needed to become part of a protected, historically significant metropolitan park, without overwhelming its landscape.

02The architectural response

An undulating concrete canopy echoes the rolling topography of the park. Strategic micro-piles elevate the structure to avoid major root systems, allowing the landscape to continue beneath the architecture.
